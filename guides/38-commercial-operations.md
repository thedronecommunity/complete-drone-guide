## Remote Pilot License (India)

    Requirements:
    ├── Age: 18+ years
    ├── Class 10 pass minimum
    ├── Valid medical certificate
    ├── Training from DGCA-approved RPTO
    ├── Clear written + practical exam
    └── Background verification

    Training includes:
    ├── Drone regulations and airspace
    ├── Aviation weather
    ├── Navigation and emergency procedures
    ├── Pre-flight planning
    ├── Practical flying (minimum hours per category)
    └── RPAS maintenance basics

    Cost: ₹25,000-₹75,000 (varies by RPTO)
    Duration: 5-15 days

## Insurance

    Types needed:
    ├── Third-party liability (mandatory)
    │   Covers damage to people/property
    │   Premium: ₹5,000-₹20,000/year per drone
    │
    ├── Hull insurance (recommended)
    │   Covers damage to your drone
    │   Premium: 5-15% of drone value per year
    │
    └── Professional liability (recommended)
        Covers errors in your work
        Premium: ₹10,000-₹50,000/year

    Providers in India:
    ├── ICICI Lombard
    ├── Bajaj Allianz
    ├── New India Assurance
    └── Specialized drone insurance brokers

## Pricing Your Services

    Pricing factors:
    ├── Equipment depreciation (drone lifespan: 2-3 years)
    ├── Battery replacement cost
    ├── Insurance premiums
    ├── Travel expenses
    ├── Post-processing time
